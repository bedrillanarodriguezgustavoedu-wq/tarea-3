Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

A few details about the demo, sample data, and getting in touch.

Does this website need a database? +

No. This version uses sample information in the app and works without PHP, Laravel, or a database. The demo form opens an email draft instead of storing or sending data from the site.

Is the clinical information real? +

No. Patient names, clinical notes, charts, and risk estimates shown here are illustrative demo content. They are not medical advice and must not be used to make care decisions.

How do I request a product demo? +

Complete the request form below. It prepares an email in your email application; you can review and send it yourself.

Can this demo be used as a medical tool? +

No. This site is a visual product demonstration only. It does not diagnose conditions, assess real patients, or replace professional medical judgment.
